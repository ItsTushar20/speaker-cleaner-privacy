# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

Speaker Cleaner: Water & Dust Remover ("the app") is provided by **Alpha Games** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the Android app.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not operate its own analytics or cloud service. Your first-run safety-notice choice and temporary audio-volume recovery information are stored on your device using Android app storage. The recovery value is used to restore the audio volume if the app did not close normally and is cleared after recovery. The app does not access your microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location.

## Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads, and to collect or manage advertising consent where required. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit. Your choices in a UMP consent message may be stored by Google and used to apply your preferences to ad requests.

Google processes this information under its own policies and terms. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or operate a service that stores app usage data. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads and UMP is processed by Google and its service providers under Google's applicable retention practices. Local preferences and recovery information remain in the app's private storage until changed, recovered, or removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Ads are not configured as child-directed.

## Your choices

You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings. Where the app displays a **Privacy options** entry, you can reopen the available consent choices there. You can also stop ad-related collection by uninstalling the app. Some ads may be non-personalized or unavailable depending on your consent choices, region, or network connection.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
